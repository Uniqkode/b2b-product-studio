ABOUT BRICKWISE

A focused AI product.
A clearly defined buyer.

For facility managers and building maintenance teams. Brickwise groups repeated defects in photos, reads technician notes and suggests which issues need a qualified inspection first.

Illustrative operating context · licensed photography
THE BUSINESS IDEA

AI reviews building inspection images and notes, then drafts an evidence-linked repair list.

A multi-building inspection generates thousands of images and notes. The AI must distinguish duplicate observations, link them to the right asset and prepare a useful issue summary.

Who buys it
Facility managers & building operators
What they send in
Inspection photos, thermal images and technician notes
What they receive
A grouped defect list and draft work orders
How it is sold
A B2B subscription with a defined processing allowance.
