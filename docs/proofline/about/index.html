ABOUT PROOFLINE

A focused AI product.
A clearly defined buyer.

For procurement and information-security teams. Proofline reads policies, questionnaires and supporting reports, then drafts a review showing what is supported, unclear or still missing.

Illustrative operating context · licensed photography
THE BUSINESS IDEA

AI compares supplier security documents with your questions and cites the answers.

A single supplier review can involve dozens of long documents and hundreds of questions. The AI must find the relevant passage, compare it with the question and identify conflicting or absent evidence.

Who buys it
Procurement & information-security teams
What they send in
Vendor policies, questionnaires and supporting reports
What they receive
A cited security review with unanswered evidence requests
How it is sold
A B2B subscription with a defined processing allowance.
