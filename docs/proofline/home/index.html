SECURITY EVIDENCE, READ CAREFULLY

A confident answer
needs a precise scope.

A single supplier review can involve dozens of long documents and hundreds of questions. The AI must find the relevant passage, compare it with the question and identify conflicting or absent evidence.

A drafted answer is not an assurance opinion or certification. A qualified reviewer decides whether the evidence is sufficient.

THE PROBLEM WORTH SOLVING

A confident answer still needs the right scope.

A supplier questionnaire may say that a control covers everyone while a policy only describes one group. A reviewer needs the exact question and supporting passage together before accepting the answer.
