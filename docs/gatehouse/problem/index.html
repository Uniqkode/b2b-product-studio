THE OPERATING PROBLEM

A complete file can still miss the job dates.

A contractor pack can contain an insurance schedule or training certificate that does not cover the planned work. The reviewer needs to compare the evidence with the actual job requirement.

01

A document can expire before the job ends.

Large sites onboard many contractors, each with several documents that expire or change. The AI reads the file, extracts the relevant dates and compares the evidence with the job requirements.

02

The stated scope may not cover the planned task.

The useful output needs the original reference and a clear next action.

03

Missing evidence needs a recorded request and an appointed decision maker.

It checks supplied documents. It does not authenticate a certificate, identify a person or independently authorize workplace access.
