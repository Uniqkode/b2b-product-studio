THE PRODUCT

AI reads contractor documents and flags missing or expired evidence for a reviewer.

For industrial workplaces and facilities teams. Gatehouse compares training certificates, insurance schedules and work-permit requirements, then explains what is missing before a contractor starts a job.

THE PRODUCT, IN USE

A file check that explains
what is still missing.

Choose a case, run the prepared analysis and open the evidence behind each finding. The decision stays with the person responsible for the work.

HOW THE JOB GETS DONE

Four steps.
One useful decision.

It checks supplied documents. It does not authenticate a certificate, identify a person or independently authorize workplace access.

  1. 01

    Read the job pack

    Read the contractor’s supplied documents and the site’s requirements.

  2. 02

    Extract the evidence

    Find the training scope, insurance dates and relevant references.

  3. 03

    Compare with the job

    Show missing evidence and dates that do not cover the planned work.

  4. 04

    Send for sign-off

    An appointed workplace manager checks the source and makes the decision.
