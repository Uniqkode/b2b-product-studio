A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Clear terms.
Before the first step.

Can I try it without paying?+

Yes. Every demo interaction is available without payment. Use the published demo credentials or the one-click demo button.

Are these existing customer plans?+

No. These are illustrative prices for evaluating the business concept. The sample workspaces do not represent real paying customers.

Does annual pricing change the allowance?+

No. The proposed annual plan changes the price, not the included allowance. The displayed monthly equivalent is 20% lower.

What would a real pilot include?+

A written scope, agreed data sources, appropriate identity controls and a success measure. The pilot brief form helps you draft that scope locally.
