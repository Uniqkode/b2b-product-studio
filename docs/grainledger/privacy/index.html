PRIVACY NOTICE

Privacy, plainly.

MVP notice · Updated 14 November 2026. Your application edits stay in this browser.

What this site is

GrainLedger is a working software MVP with a browser-local workspace. This notice describes its current data handling and optional connected AI service. Production identity and shared data services are part of the roadmap.

Information entered in the demo

Sample records are included in the website source. New records and edited notes are saved in localStorage. A demo session flag is saved in sessionStorage. Accepted analysis reports and pilot brief drafts are also saved in localStorage. User-uploaded image bytes are not saved with accepted reports. Prepared-example interactions do not send the entered content to an application server. The optional connected-analysis form sends its text and selected images to the service address you specify when you choose Analyze with connected service. That service can send them to its configured AI provider. Its retention and access policies apply.

Passwords and account information

The visible demo password is a public example. It is checked in the browser and is never stored by the application. Do not enter a password used for another service. The demo email does not create an account.

Hosting and external services

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Ordinary requests may be logged by the hosting provider under its privacy policy. Photographs and font files are included locally. This implementation adds no analytics, advertising pixels, cookies or third-party form processors.

Retention and deletion

Saved draft, record and accepted analysis data remains until you clear browser storage or use Reset demo data. Closing the tab ends the session flag. Reset affects this brand in the current browser. It does not delete downloaded CSV or brief files.

Your choices

Use sample content only. You can explore without entering personal information by choosing the one-click demo. You can export records, download a pilot brief and reset saved data. This demonstration should be replaced by a reviewed privacy notice before any production service processes real personal information.
