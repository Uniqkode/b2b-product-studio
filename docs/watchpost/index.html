WHY THIS NEEDS SUBSTANTIAL AI PROCESSING

Real work creates
a recurring workload.

Video is a continuous workload. The system checks frames for defined events, groups repeated detections and sends only the relevant time window for a supervisor to review.

Planning scenario · not current customer usageRepeated checks while the site is monitored
57,024,000frame checks / month
9,504,000stream-minute windows in this scenario
6sampled frames per minute
55 organizations×8 camera streams per operator×21,600 monitored minutes / stream / month×6 sampled frames per minute

The scenario assumes 12 hours per day for 30 days. Sampling six frames per minute produces a large recurring computer-vision workload; full clips are escalated only for defined events.

HOW THE JOB GETS DONE

Four steps.
One useful decision.

No face identification or automatic emergency dispatch. A supervisor verifies every event and follows the site’s response procedure.

  1. 01

    Set the site rules

    Choose the cameras, operating hours and events the site is authorized to monitor.

  2. 02

    Check the footage

    Look for the defined event and group repeated detections.

  3. 03

    Show the relevant clip

    Attach the camera reference, time window and reason for review.

  4. 04

    Verify and respond

    A supervisor checks the video and records the response.
