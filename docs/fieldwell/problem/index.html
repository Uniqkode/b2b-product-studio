THE OPERATING PROBLEM

The survey is ready. The review takes longer.

A field survey can arrive as hundreds of overlapping images. An agronomist still has to find the relevant area, compare it with the last visit and give the scout a useful next action.

01

The same stressed area appears in several photographs.

One drone survey can contain hundreds of overlapping images. The useful job is to compare new observations with the last survey and group repeated signs of stress into areas a field team can inspect.

02

A change in crop colour needs the previous survey for context.

The useful output needs the original reference and a clear next action.

03

A field visit needs an image, a location and a clear question.

It supports scouting. A photograph alone cannot confirm disease, nutrient deficiency or a treatment.
